## Starting Position
1. **Rings**: Set the rings low, so you can hold them with straight arms while kneeling.
2. **Knees**: Kneel on a pad with your knees under your hips.
3. **Body**: Tuck your pelvis, pull your ribs down and squeeze your glutes so your body forms a straight line from knees to head.

## Movement
1. **Rollout Phase**:
   - Lean forward from the knees and let the rings move forward and overhead.
   - Keep your arms straight and your hips extended.

2. **Return Phase**:
   - Pull the rings back toward your hips with your abs until you are upright on your knees.

## Key Points
- Do not bend at the hips. The line from knees to head stays straight.
- Stop the rollout before your lower back starts to sag.
- Move on to the standing Ring Ab Rollout once 3 sets of 12 feel controlled.

### Kneel Under the Rings
Set the rings low and kneel on a pad, holding them with straight arms.

### Brace Your Core
Tuck your pelvis, pull your ribs down and squeeze your glutes.

### Roll Out
Lean forward from the knees and let the rings move forward, keeping your arms straight and body in one line.
