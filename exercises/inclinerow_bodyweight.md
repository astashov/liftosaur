## Starting Position
1. **Grip**: Hold a bar at chest height, or a pair of rings, with an overhand grip about shoulder-width apart.
2. **Feet**: Walk your feet forward so your body leans back at about 45 degrees, heels on the floor.
3. **Body**: Straighten your arms and hold a straight line from heels to head, glutes and core tight.

## Movement
1. **Pulling Phase**:
   - Pull your chest to the bar, keeping your elbows close to your body.
   - Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the top.

2. **Lowering Phase**:
   - Lower yourself under control until your arms are straight.

## Key Points
- Keep your body straight. Do not let your hips sag or pike.
- Walk your feet further forward, or lower the bar, to get closer to horizontal and make it harder.
- Move on to horizontal inverted rows once you can do 3 sets of 8 close to horizontal.

### Set the Angle
Hold a chest-high bar or rings and walk your feet forward until your body leans back at about 45 degrees.

### Brace Your Body
Straighten your arms and hold a straight line from heels to head with your glutes and core tight.

### Pull to the Bar
Pull your chest to the bar with your elbows close to your body and squeeze your shoulder blades together.

### Lower With Control
Lower yourself until your arms are fully straight, keeping your body in a straight line.
