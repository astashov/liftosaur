## Starting Position
1. **Body**: Lie on your back with your arms pointing straight up at the ceiling.
2. **Legs**: Lift your legs with the hips and knees bent to 90 degrees.
3. **Lower Back**: Press your lower back into the floor and brace your core.

## Movement
1. **Extending Phase**:
   - Slowly lower one arm overhead and straighten the opposite leg toward the floor.
   - Stop just above the floor, keeping the lower back down.

2. **Returning Phase**:
   - Bring the arm and leg back to the start, then repeat on the other side.

## Key Points
- Move slowly. Speed hides a lower back that lifts off the floor.
- Breathe out as you extend.
- Only go as low as you can while the lower back stays flat.

### Set Up on Your Back
Lie on your back with your arms pointing up and your hips and knees bent to 90 degrees.

### Brace Your Core
Press your lower back into the floor and tighten your abs.

### Extend Opposite Limbs
Slowly lower one arm overhead and straighten the opposite leg until both hover just above the floor.

### Return and Switch
Bring them back to the start and repeat with the other arm and leg.
