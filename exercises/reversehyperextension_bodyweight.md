## Starting Position
1. **Body**: Lie face down on a bench, table or box with your hips at the edge and your legs hanging toward the floor.
2. **Grip**: Hold the sides or the far end of the bench to keep your upper body in place.
3. **Legs**: Keep your legs together and straight, toes lightly touching the floor.

## Movement
1. **Lifting Phase**:
   - Squeeze your glutes and lift both legs behind you until they are in line with your torso.
   - Keep your legs straight and lift with your hips, not by arching your lower back.

2. **Lowering Phase**:
   - Lower your legs under control back toward the floor.

## Key Points
- Lift to a straight line with your body. Swinging higher only arches the lower back.
- Pause for a moment at the top to make the glutes do the work.
- Once 3 sets of 12 are easy, add ankle weights or hold a dumbbell between your feet.

### Lie on the Bench
Lie face down with your hips at the edge of a bench, table or box, and hold on to keep your upper body still.

### Let the Legs Hang
Keep your legs straight and together, hanging toward the floor.

### Lift Your Legs
Squeeze your glutes and lift both legs until they are in line with your body, then pause.

### Lower Under Control
Lower your legs slowly back toward the floor without swinging.
