## Starting Position
1. **Band**: Anchor a resistance band high behind you, at a wall bar, rack or door anchor, and hold the other end at your chest or behind your head.
2. **Knees**: Kneel on a pad with your knees about hip-width apart.
3. **Ankles**: Anchor your ankles under something sturdy, about a fist's distance above the ankle bone.
4. **Body**: Extend your hips so your torso and thighs form a straight line, glutes squeezed and core braced.

## Movement
1. **Lowering Phase**:
   - Keeping your hips extended, lower your torso toward the floor as slowly as you can.
   - Let the band take part of the load, but keep the hamstrings working the whole way down.
   - Do not let your hips fold or hinge back.

2. **Raising Phase**:
   - For negatives, catch yourself with your hands at the bottom and push back up to the start.
   - For full reps, pull yourself back up with your hamstrings, using the band for help.

## Key Points
- Start with negatives: lower slowly, then push back up with your hands.
- Move to full reps once 3 sets of 8 slow negatives feel solid.
- Use a lighter band as you get stronger, until you can do Nordic Curls without one.
- Keep a straight line from knees to head. Piking at the hips takes the work off the hamstrings.

### Set Up the Band
Anchor a resistance band high behind you and hold the free end at your chest or behind your head.

### Anchor Your Ankles
Kneel on a pad and hook your ankles under something sturdy, with your hips extended and glutes squeezed.

### Lower Slowly
Keep a straight line from knees to head and lower your torso toward the floor as slowly as you can, letting the band take part of your weight.

### Return to the Top
Push back up with your hands for a negative, or pull yourself up with your hamstrings for a full rep.
