## Starting Position
1. **Grip**: Hang from a bar with a wide overhand grip, about twice shoulder width.
2. **Body**: Start from a dead hang with straight arms and a slightly hollow body.

## Movement
1. **Pulling Phase**:
   - Pull your chin toward one hand, bending only that arm.
   - Keep the other arm as straight as you can, pushing it along the bar.

2. **Lowering Phase**:
   - Lower under control back to the dead hang.
   - Switch sides on the next rep, or do all reps on one side, then the other.

## Key Points
- The assisting arm stays straight. That is what makes it an archer pull-up.
- Rest the assisting hand on top of the bar to make it easier.
- Do the same number of reps on each side.

<!-- howto -->

### Take a Wide Grip
Hang from a bar with an overhand grip about twice shoulder width and straight arms.

### Pull to One Side
Pull your chin toward one hand, bending only that arm.

### Keep the Other Arm Straight
Push the assisting arm along the bar and keep it as straight as possible.

### Lower and Switch
Lower under control to a dead hang, then repeat toward the other hand.
