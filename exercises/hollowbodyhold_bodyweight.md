## Starting Position
1. **Body**: Lie on your back with your arms overhead and legs straight.
2. **Lower Back**: Tuck your pelvis and press your lower back into the floor.

## Movement
1. **Lifting Phase**:
   - Lift your shoulders, arms and legs a few inches off the floor.
   - Keep your lower back pressed down and your ribs pulled in.

2. **Holding Phase**:
   - Hold the banana-shaped position for time while breathing steadily.

## Key Points
- The lower back stays on the floor. If it lifts, make the hold easier.
- To make it easier, bend your knees into a tuck or bring your arms to your sides.
- To make it harder, extend your arms overhead and lower your legs closer to the floor.

### Lie on Your Back
Lie flat with your arms overhead and your legs straight.

### Press Your Lower Back Down
Tuck your pelvis and press your lower back firmly into the floor.

### Lift and Hold
Lift your shoulders, arms and legs off the floor and hold, keeping your lower back down.

### Adjust the Difficulty
Bend your knees or bring your arms in to make it easier, or reach further and lower your legs to make it harder.
