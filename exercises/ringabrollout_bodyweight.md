## Starting Position
1. **Rings**: Set the rings at about hip height.
2. **Grip**: Stand close to the rings and hold them with straight arms in front of your thighs.
3. **Body**: Tuck your pelvis, pull your ribs down and squeeze your glutes into a hollow body position.

## Movement
1. **Rollout Phase**:
   - Lean forward and let the rings travel forward and up overhead as your body lowers.
   - Keep your arms straight and your body in one straight line.

2. **Return Phase**:
   - Pull the rings back down toward your hips with your abs and lats until you are standing again.

## Key Points
- Keep the hollow body position the whole time. A sagging lower back means you went too far.
- Higher rings make it easier. Lower rings or elevated feet make it harder.
- Keep your hands as close together as your shoulder mobility allows.
