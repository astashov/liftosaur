## Starting Position
1. **Grip**: Hang from gymnastics rings or a bar with a shoulder-width grip and straight arms.
2. **Body**: Start in a dead hang with your legs together.

## Movement
1. **Tuck Phase**:
   - Lift your knees to your chest and roll your hips backward, passing your legs between your arms.
   - Keep your arms straight.

2. **German Hang Phase**:
   - Continue rotating until your feet point toward the floor behind you and your shoulders are stretched.
   - Lower only as far as your shoulder mobility allows.

3. **Return Phase**:
   - Pull your knees back through your arms and reverse the movement to the dead hang.

## Key Points
- Move slowly. The bottom position puts a large stretch on the shoulders.
- Keep the arms straight the whole way.
- The r/bodyweightfitness wiki asks for 5 sets of 2 before you start the Tuck Front Lever row path.

### Hang From the Rings
Take a shoulder-width grip on the rings or a bar and start in a dead hang with straight arms.

### Tuck and Rotate
Lift your knees to your chest and roll backward, passing your legs between your arms.

### Lower Into the German Hang
Keep rotating until your feet point down behind you, stopping where your shoulders allow.

### Return to the Start
Tuck your knees back through your arms and reverse the movement under control.
