## Starting Position
1. **Grip**: Hold a light resistance band with both hands, much wider than shoulder width.
2. **Body**: Stand tall with the band in front of your hips and your arms straight.

## Movement
1. **Overhead Phase**:
   - Raise the band in front of you and over your head, keeping your arms straight.

2. **Behind Phase**:
   - Continue the arc until the band touches your lower back.

3. **Return Phase**:
   - Reverse the movement back over your head to the front of your hips.

## Key Points
- Keep the arms straight. Bending the elbows removes the stretch.
- Start with a wide grip and bring the hands closer only as your shoulders allow.
- Move slowly and keep your ribs down, without arching your lower back.
