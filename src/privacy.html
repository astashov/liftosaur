1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information you provide to us

In Short: We collect the account details you give us and the training data you record in the app.

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you create an account, use the Services, or contact us. This includes:

  • Account information — your email address, and the identifier from the sign-in provider you choose (Google or Apple).
  • Training data — the programs you write and the workouts you record, including for each set the exercise, equipment, weight, reps, RPE, rest and set timers, and the times at which you logged them.
  • Body measurements — body weight, body fat, and any length measurements you choose to enter, such as chest, waist, or limb circumference. These are optional and may be left empty.
  • Correspondence — the contents of any message you send us.

Your training data is the core of the Services, and we store it so that it stays available to you across your devices. See section 3 for how we use it.

Information automatically collected

In Short: Some information - such as IP address and/or browser and device characteristics - is collected automatically when you visit our Services.

We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use or navigate the Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name or contact information) but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, language preferences, referring URLs, device name, country, location, information about how and when you use our Services and other technical information. This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our Services, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.

Like many businesses, we also collect information through cookies and similar technologies.

2. APPLE HEALTH AND GOOGLE HEALTH CONNECT INTEGRATIONS

Our app can integrate with Apple Health on iOS and Google Health Connect on Android, if you grant it permission. Both integrations are optional, you choose which categories to allow, and you can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app or in Health Connect. What we read and write is the same on both platforms, with one exception noted below:

  • Body measurements — read and written. Body weight and body fat are synced in both directions, so that measurements you record in Liftosaur appear in Health and vice versa. On iOS we also sync waist circumference; Health Connect does not offer it, so on Android we do not.
  • Workouts — written only. When you finish a workout, we write it as a workout session, together with an estimate of active energy burned, so that your training shows up alongside the rest of your activity. We do not read workouts back.
  • Sleep and nutrition — read only. We read sleep duration and dietary calories and protein, to display them alongside your workouts. We never write sleep or nutrition data back.

Here's how we handle this data:

1. Data Collection

We collect data through the Apple Health and Google Health Connect integrations to enhance the app's functionality. This data is used solely for tracking and providing insights within the app.

2. Data Storage

The collected data is stored locally on your device and in the cloud on our servers. We implement strict security measures to protect this data from unauthorized access, ensuring it is stored safely and securely.

3. Data Usage

The data read from Apple Health or Google Health Connect is not used for any purpose other than to provide app functionality. We do not use it for marketing or advertising, and we do not include it in the aggregated statistics described in section 3.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your health data to third parties. Your data is private and is only accessible within the app for your use.

5. User Control

You retain control over your data. You can withdraw any permission at any time in the Apple Health app or in Health Connect, which stops further syncing in both directions. You can delete or update your body measurements at any time within the app. Sleep and nutrition records are read-only mirrors: you can hide any imported day from view in the app (Me -> Sleep & Nutrition), and you manage or remove the underlying records in Apple Health or Health Connect itself. Workouts we have written are yours to delete there too. If you wish to delete your data stored on our servers, you can do that through the app's settings — see section 10.

6. Compliance

We comply with all applicable privacy laws and regulations related to the use and storage of health data.

3. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We process your information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our contract with you, compliance with our legal obligations, and/or your consent.

We use personal information collected via our Services for a variety of business purposes described below. We process your personal information for these purposes in reliance on our legitimate business interests, in order to enter into or perform a contract with you, with your consent, and/or for compliance with our legal obligations.

  • To provide and operate the Services — create and maintain your account, store your programs and workout history, and keep them in sync across your devices.

  • To keep the Services working and secure — monitor errors and crashes, prevent abuse, and protect against fraud.
  • To improve the Services — understand which features are used and where people run into trouble, so that we can make the app better.

  • To measure and improve our marketing — as described in sections 5 and 6.
  • To comply with our legal obligations — including responding to lawful requests and enforcing our terms.

5. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

Yes. We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to collect information about how you use our site and app. We group them into three categories:

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site and your account to work. Always on.
  • Analytics — help us understand usage and which page first brought you to us (Google Analytics).
  • Advertising — measure and improve our ads (Reddit Pixel, Google Ads, and campaign/UTM parameters).

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before setting any non-essential (analytics or advertising) cookies. Elsewhere, these are enabled by default and you may opt out. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time using the Privacy Settings link in our footer, and we automatically honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal.

Key cookies and similar storage we use:

  • lft_landing (analytics, first-party) — records the first page you arrived on; up to 12 months.
  • _ga, _ga_* (analytics, Google) — distinguish users; up to 24 months.
  • _gid (analytics, Google) — distinguish users; 24 hours.
  • _rdt_uuid (advertising, Reddit) — ad measurement; up to 3 months.
  • _gcl_au (advertising, Google) — ad conversion measurement; up to 90 days.

6. ANALYTICS AND ADVERTISING TECHNOLOGIES

We use analytics and advertising services to understand how visitors interact with Liftosaur and to improve our marketing. These include:

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We keep your personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes set out in this notice, unless a longer period is required by law. In practice:

  • Your account and training data — kept for as long as your account exists. Your workout history is the point of the app, so we do not expire or trim it: workouts you recorded years ago stay available to you.
  • After you delete your account — removed from our live systems straight away. See section 10 for what happens to backup copies.
  • Aggregated, de-identified statistics — these no longer identify anyone, and may be kept and published indefinitely. They are not affected by deleting your account, and cannot be traced back to you.
  • Cookies and similar storage — for the periods listed in section 5.
  • Records held by analytics and advertising providers — retained according to those providers' own schedules, which we do not control. Section 6 links to their opt-outs.

8. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or elsewhere outside the United States, this means your information is transferred to and stored in the United States. Where the law of your country requires a safeguard for that transfer, we rely on the appropriate safeguards available to us under applicable data protection law — currently the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, and the UK Addendum to them, which form part of our data processing agreement with Amazon Web Services.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information. However, please remember that no internet transmission is 100% secure.

10. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

You may review, change, or delete your information at any time. You can delete your account and its data from the app's account settings, or by contacting us at info@liftosaur.com.

When you delete your account, we immediately remove your programs, workout history, body measurements, uploaded images, subscription records and account details from our live systems. Residual copies remain in our encrypted database backups for up to 35 days, after which they are overwritten on our normal backup cycle. We do not access or use backup copies for any other purpose in the meantime.

Two things are not affected by deleting your account: aggregated statistics that no longer identify you (see section 3), and records held by the analytics and advertising providers listed in section 6, which you can opt out of separately.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, correct it, object to or restrict how we process it, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Email info@liftosaur.com to exercise any of these.

11. DO-NOT-TRACK AND GLOBAL PRIVACY CONTROL

Most browsers' legacy "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature has no common standard, so we do not respond to DNT signals. We do, however, honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC): when your browser or an extension sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out of advertising and of any "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information.

12. DO CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Yes. California residents — and residents of other U.S. states with similar privacy laws — have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can exercise this using the Privacy Settings link in our footer, or by enabling Global Privacy Control (GPC) in your browser, which we honor automatically. We do not sell personal information for money. California residents may also request details of personal data shared for marketing under the "Shine The Light" law by contacting us at info@liftosaur.com.
